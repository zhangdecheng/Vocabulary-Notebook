P: After work, they went shopping in the mall. Leo needed a new {phone charger|701}. Ana wanted {AirPods|703} for her birthday. Leo tried on a shirt, but the {size|700} was wrong. P: At six, it was time to {depart|710}. The traffic in Denpasar was terrible. "{Good luck|1178} on the road!" Warren said. Leo smiled. "I don't need luck. I need a helicopter."
Z: 六点，该出发回去了。登巴萨的交通糟透了。“路上祝你好运！”Warren 说。Leo 笑了：“我不需要运气，我需要一架直升机。”
