# 57 | ⑤ 食物与饮食 | Cooking Night with Chef Warren | Warren 大厨的烹饪之夜
P: Warren used to work as a {chef|320} in Sydney. On Friday night, he cooked for the whole house. "Tonight we have {mussels|313}, chicken {curry|319} and an apple {pie|318}," he said. Leo was surprised. "You can cook all that?"
Z: Warren 以前在悉尼当过厨师。周五晚上，他给全屋的人做饭。“今晚我们有青口贝、鸡肉咖喱，还有苹果派，”他说。Leo 很惊讶：“这些你都会做？”
P: Leo helped with the curry. Warren showed him the steps. "First, {mix up|322} the chicken and the spices. Then add the {salt|311}. Not too much. Nobody likes {salty|310} curry." Leo tasted it. "It needs a little {sugar|312}," he said. Warren agreed.
Z: Leo 帮忙做咖喱。Warren 教他步骤：“先把鸡肉和香料拌匀，然后放盐。别放太多，没人喜欢太咸的咖喱。”Leo 尝了尝：“还需要一点糖，”他说。Warren 表示同意。
P: Kenji came late with a big {bucket|321} of {fried|316} chicken from a {delivery shop|324}. "Sorry, I was hungry," he said. He also brought chips and {tomato sauce|314}. "In Australia we say tomato sauce, not ketchup," Warren said.
Z: Kenji 来晚了，带着一大桶从外卖店买来的炸鸡。“不好意思，我饿了，”他说。他还带了薯条和番茄酱。“在澳大利亚我们说 tomato sauce，不说 ketchup，”Warren 说。
P: Then Warren used a small {fryer|315} to make banana {fritters|317}, like the pisang goreng on the street. "Out of the fryer, add a little sugar on top," he said. Ketut ate three of them.
Z: 接着 Warren 用一个小油炸锅做了香蕉炸饼，就像街边的炸香蕉一样。“从炸锅里拿出来后，在上面撒一点糖，”他说。Ketut 一口气吃了三个。
P: After dinner, everyone sat outside. Ana made a lemon and mint drink. "This is so {refreshing|323}," Leo said. "Good food, good friends and a cool drink. That's a perfect Friday."
Z: 晚饭后，大家坐在外面。Ana 做了一杯柠檬薄荷饮料。“这个好清爽，”Leo 说，“好吃的、好朋友，再加一杯凉饮，这就是完美的周五。”
