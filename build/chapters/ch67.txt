# 67 | ⑯ 时间与日程 | Birthdays and Holidays | 生日和节日
P: Sonia wrote "{Dates|1273}" in a big circle. "What is the date today?" she asked. "It's the twenty-third of October," Ana said. "Good. For dates we use {first, second, third … thirty-first|1283}. Not 'October twenty-three'." Then she asked Leo, "{When were you born|1282}?" "On {November|1271} 5th," Leo said.
Z: Sonia 在一个大圆圈里写了“日期”。“今天是几号？”她问。“十月二十三号，”Ana 说。“很好。日期要用序数词：first、second、third……thirty-first，不要说 ‘October twenty-three’。”然后她问 Leo：“你是哪天出生的？”“十一月五号，”Leo 说。
P: Sonia asked another question: "{If you celebrate your birthday in Bali, what will you do?|1179}" Leo thought. "I will go to the beach with my friends and eat a big cake." Kenji said, "I will go to a fight night!" Everybody laughed.
Z: Sonia 又问了一个问题：“如果你在巴厘岛过生日，你会做什么？”Leo 想了想：“我会和朋友们去海滩，吃一个大蛋糕。”Kenji 说：“我会去看格斗之夜！”大家都笑了。
P: Next, they talked about months. "{May|1275} is the fifth month, {June|1276} is the sixth, and {July|1277} is the seventh," Sonia said. "In China, {Labor Day|1280} is in May and {National Day|1281} is in October," Leo said. "Here, National Day is on August 17th," Pak Made said. He was listening at the door.
Z: 接着他们聊到月份。“五月是第五个月，六月是第六个，七月是第七个，”Sonia 说。“在中国，劳动节在五月，国庆节在十月，”Leo 说。“我们这里的国庆节是八月十七日，”Pak Made 说，他正在门口听着。
P: "In Bali, there is a {festival|1278} almost every month," Pak Made said. "On big festival days, schools and offices close. It's a {day off|1279}." "We say day off or off day," Sonia added. "Both are fine."
Z: “在巴厘岛，几乎每个月都有节日，”Pak Made 说，“大节日那天，学校和办公室都关门，放一天假。”“我们说 day off 或 off day，”Sonia 补充道，“两种都可以。”
P: At the end, Sonia gave homework: write about "{the case of life deep impression in different country|1183}." Leo wrote: "Living in a different country left a deep impression on me. Every day in Bali feels like a festival."
Z: 最后，Sonia 布置了作业：写“在异国生活留下的深刻印象”。Leo 写道：“在另一个国家生活给我留下了很深的印象。在巴厘岛的每一天都像过节一样。”
