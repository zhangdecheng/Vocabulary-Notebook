## 第 1 章 · 周一：新的学习计划（11/02 课）

> 段意：Leo 想去国外找工作，先和老师 Ms. Rina 重新制定学习方法：少被动灌输，多自学和独立思考。

It is Monday, the first day of a new **week**. Leo has a big plan: he wants to find a job abroad. But first, his English needs to get better.

"Let's **warm up**," says Ms. Rina, his English teacher. "**Talk** with Ken for five minutes about your weekend."

After the warm-up, she shows Leo a chart. "This is the **CEFR** scale. It can **guide** you. You are at B1 now. Your goal is B2."

Leo is honest. "At my old school, everything was **spoon-fed**. The teacher talked, and we copied."

"Many **ESL** learners **say** that," Ms. Rina answers. "But copying is not a good **study method**. You also need **self-study** and **independent thinking**."

"Sometimes my old classroom felt like a **prison**," Leo laughs. "And homework felt like a **punishment**."

"Not here," she smiles. "Let's **research** what will **suit** you. Then we can **arrange** your day in three **parts**." Together they **set** a **fixed** time for **practice**: one hour every morning.

"You don't need to **master** everything this month," she says. "But please don't be **sloppy**. Check your spelling before you send your homework."

Ken joins them. "I learn English from a **Korean drama** with English subtitles. My cousin learns **French** the same way."

"Good idea," says Ms. Rina. "Maybe you two can be a study **team**."

After one more short **bout** of speaking practice, Leo looks at his notebook. He sees a word from an earlier lesson: **Mewali**. "What does this mean?" he asks. "It's a Balinese word in our class notes," Ms. Rina says. "I'm not sure of the exact meaning. Let's ask a Balinese friend before we use it."

That night, Leo posts a **story** on Instagram about his new plan. "**Share** your favorite study tips!" he writes. Many friends **like** it, some **save** it, and ten new people **follow** him.


## 第 2 章 · 周二：一份海外求职申请（11/03 课）

> 段意：课堂模拟海外求职：选城市、填网申表、谈优缺点和性格，再了解五步招聘流程。


### 2.1 Choosing a city

On Tuesday, Ms. Rina asks, "Imagine you **apply for** a job in another country. How do you choose the city?"

Ken answers first. "A **high salary**, **of course**! I **love** big cities."

Leo thinks about it. "Money is one **factor**. But I also think about the **weather**, **air pollution**, and the **rent**. A **low living cost** is important to me. It's hard to make a **living** in a very expensive city."

"In Bali, I can **ride** my scooter everywhere," he adds. "The only problem is the mosquitoes. I always have a lot of **mosquito bites**, and my arms are **itchy**."

"So for you, **happiness** matters more than money?" Ms. Rina asks. "I want **both**," Leo laughs. "Good money and a happy life can **complement each other**."


### 2.2 The online form

Then they practice with a sample online application form. "First, upload your **résumé** as a **PDF**," Ms. Rina says. "Then fill in your **profile**."

The **system** asks for Leo's **nationality**, **location**, and **gender**. On this form, there are only two boxes: **male** and **female**.

The next **item** asks, "Are you **pregnant**?" Leo stops. "Why do they need this? It's private." He is worried about his privacy, so he leaves it blank. "You can also email the **admin** and ask why they need it," says Ms. Rina.

"Also read the **requirements**," she says. "Some jobs need a university **diploma** and English test results. And check the news. Is there a **visa ban** for people from your country?"

Leo checks everything. Then he clicks **submit**. A minute later, an email arrives: "Please **confirm** your email address."


### 2.3 Strengths and weaknesses

"Now, the **interview**," says Ms. Rina. "Every **applicant** hears this question: What are your **strengths and weaknesses**?"

Leo tries. "I have a calm **personality**, and I always keep a **tidy** desk. My weakness? I can be **stubborn**." "That's honest," she says. "Being stubborn is a **risk** in a team. But it also shows that you don't give up."

"Companies like people with a **stable** work history," she adds. "And they don't want someone **unreliable**."

Ken grins. "My **zodiac** sign is **Taurus**. Its symbol is a bull. People say Taurus people are slow but hard-working!"

"A bull? Not a **cow**?" Leo jokes. Ms. Rina laughs. "Some people think each sign can **represent** a type of personality. For this interview practice, let's focus on your skills instead."

"Then I'll talk about my dog," says Ken. "He's my most **faithful** friend. He waits for me at the door every day."


### 2.4 Different paths

Ms. Rina tells them about her old students. "One was an **artist**. He loved **art** and wanted to work in the **entertainment industry**. He went to an **acting school** first. Later, a **theatre academy** in London **admitted** him."

"Another was a **programmer**. He asked me, 'Am I **too old** to change jobs at forty?' I said no. I always **encourage** people to try. Every path is **special**."

"My **schoolmate** became a **salesperson** at a shoe company," Ken says. "Last year a famous singer agreed to **endorse** the shoes. Some fans called the singer a **sellout**, because the shoes were really ugly!"


### 2.5 Five steps

Ms. Rina draws a line on the board. She writes "Apply" **above** the line and "Get the job" **below** it. "Competition for good jobs is **intense**," she says. "For our practice, let's imagine a company with these five steps."

1. An **English level exam**. It has a **listening** part and a speaking part. In the speaking part, you are just **talking** with an examiner.

2. A **personality test**.

3. A **medical check**.

4. Interviews with **HR**, a **team leader**, a **manager**, and sometimes the **HR director** or even the **CEO**.

5. **Finally**, you **receive an offer**.

"Who makes the final decision?" Leo asks. "Usually the manager is **in charge** of it," she says. "The process can **involve** several interviews and take weeks. Keep your **target** in mind. Good **communication** helps at every step, and every interview is a chance for **growth**."

After class, Leo prints his résumé and puts it on the **highest** shelf in his room, safe from the rain.


## 第 3 章 · 周三：合同、学校和闷热的房间（11/04 课）

> 段意：谈拿到 offer 之后的试用期和合同，回忆学生时代的科目，吐槽闷热的房间，并识别一个可疑的订房网站。


### 3.1 After the offer

On Wednesday, Leo asks, "What happens after I **sign a contract**?"

"**Normally**, this job starts with a **probationary period**," Ms. Rina says. "We can also call it **probation**. In this context, probationary period is a **synonym** for probation. The length depends on the employer and local rules. You get **training**, and your manager will **measure** your progress."

"How do they measure it?" "With an **evaluation**. Your manager gives you **feedback** and may write a short **report**. If you pass probation, you can keep working there. Later, when your contract is near its end, the company may **extend your contract**."

"Can I ask the **interviewer** questions too?" Leo asks. "Yes, please do. You can also **view** reviews of the company online."

"Don't **underestimate** the job, and don't **overestimate** yourself," she adds. "Before you sign, check the **amount** you will be paid and the **scope** of the job. Ask yourself: Is it **worth** it? If something feels **unfair**, you can say no."


### 3.2 School days

Then they talk about **school**. Ken grew up in **mainland** China. "At my high school, we chose **humanities** or **science**. I chose humanities. My favorite subject was **politics**."

Leo chose science: **math**, **physics**, **biology**, and **chemistry**. "And **English**, too. But I was a **bit** lazy."

"Did your grades **affect** your university **admission**?" Ms. Rina asks. "Yes. My **rank** in the exam was not very high," Leo says. "But it didn't decide my **fate**."

"Later, I studied at an art **academy**. I took a **studio course** in **dance**. Each **basic movement** could **take** weeks to learn."

"Who **influenced** you the most?" "My dance teacher. She had a soft **voice**, but her **discipline** was amazing. Her teaching **method** was simple: practice every day. Her **program** changed my life."

"Would you like to study at a **foreign** university one day?" Ken asks. "Maybe," says Leo.


### 3.3 The hot room

Leo talks about his room. "It's **hot and humid**. There's no **ventilation**, and the room is **full of** mosquitoes. I don't like **chemical** sprays, so I bought an electric **zapper**. I swing it like a racket on a tennis **court**."

"Do you have much **furniture**?" "Just a bed and a **steel** chair. It's not very **comfortable**. On the **wall**, there's a picture of a yellow **duck**. In my family, it's a **symbol** of good luck."


### 3.4 A suspicious website

"Last week I had a **bad experience**," Leo says. "I wanted a **5-star** hotel near the **beach**. I saw a great price on a website. But before I could **finish** booking, it asked for a lot of **personal information**. I had a strange **feeling**. It was **suspicious**."

"Some people buy hotel rooms in **bulk** and **sell** them again," Ms. Rina says. "Some sites are just scams. Always **swipe** through real reviews on a trusted app first."

Ken tells them about his father. "My dad works at a car **factory**." "You can also say a car **plant**," says Ms. Rina.

"Last night we watched a boxing movie," Ken adds. "The boxer's **enemy** paid him to **take a dive**. That means to lose the fight on purpose." "In the movie, it's exciting," says Ms. Rina. "In **reality**, it's cheating."


## 第 4 章 · 周四：旅行计划（11/05 课）

> 段意：Leo 通过了网申第一步，和 Ken 计划一次小旅行庆祝，聊目的地、交通费用和想家的心情。

On Thursday, Leo gets an email: he passed the first step! To **celebrate**, he and Ken plan a short trip.

"Where should we go?" Ken asks. "Maybe **Vietnam**? I want to see **historical places** and try the local **restaurants**."

"**I don't know right now**," says Leo. "Honestly, **I want to relax**. I want somewhere **peaceful**."

"How do we get there?" "**Plane tickets** are expensive this month. A **bus** is cheaper, but it **takes longer**." "We could **hire** a car." "Then we have to pay the **fuel cost**."

"Let's **buy tickets** for **public** transport, then. It's cheaper," says Leo. "And I need to **earn** a little more money first."

Ken looks quiet. "I'm a bit **homesick**. When I miss home, I lose my **appetite**."

"That's normal," says Ms. Rina. "Last year, two **Finns** in my class felt the same. They missed the snow! One of them even said our old classroom had a **ghost**." She laughs. "It was just the wind."

Then she gives Leo's homework a good **mark**.

At home, Leo opens his notes app and **creates a new list** for the trip. He looks at his week. "**I feel satisfied**," he says.


## 第 5 章 · 周五：片场、演唱会和一次求婚（11/06 课）

> 段意：晒伤和纹身的小插曲，参观片场学到 “fire in the hole” 的真实含义，晚上海边公园演唱会上遇到一场求婚。


### 5.1 Sunburn and a tattoo

On Friday, Leo has a **sunburn** on his **neck**. "I stayed at the beach too long without sun **protection**." "Wear a hat. You need to **protect** your skin," says Ms. Rina. Leo's skin is a little **darker** now.

Ken shows them a new **tattoo**. "My mom tried to **persuade** me not to get it. She says I'm **rebellious**, a real **rebel**." He smiles. "Well, **it is what it is**."

Leo says, "I very like it." Ms. Rina gently **corrects** him: "We say, 'I really like it.'"


### 5.2 On a film set

In the afternoon, they visit a small film set. Ken's cousin works there. A **handsome** actor is practicing for an action scene, and the crew is ready to **shoot** it. In the scene, a soldier has to **throw** a fake bomb into a **hole**. The actor shouts, "**Fire in the hole**!"

The director explains, "In real life, workers shout this as a warning before an explosion. Here, it's only acting, and everything is controlled. Please don't shout it for fun." Then the fake explosion makes a lot of **noise**. "That was **crazy**!" says Ken.


### 5.3 The concert

That evening, they go to a **concert** in a **park** near the beach. They **carry** chairs to a good **viewing spot**. A **strong** wind blows from the sea. **A guy at the concert** sells grilled corn, and they buy two.

**Suddenly**, the music stops. A man walks onto the stage with his girlfriend. He holds a small box in one hand and **holds** her hand with the other. Inside the box is a **gold** **ring** with a small **diamond**. It's a **proposal**! She says yes. The crowd cheers **loudly**, and the singer shouts, "**Peace and love**!"

"Perfect **timing**!" says Leo. Later, the video of the proposal gets a lot of **praise** online. Leo and Ken **pose** for a photo in front of the stage. "**Hopefully**, one day I'll **invite** someone special to a concert like this," says Leo.


### 5.4 Home again

Back home, Leo is hungry. He decides to **cook** a piece of fish. He uses the **broil** setting on his small oven. The heat comes from the top, and the fish is ready in eight minutes.

After dinner, the street outside is **empty** and quiet. Leo wants to **lie down**. He writes in his diary: "**In my free time**, I want to visit more **places** like this. This is a good **life**."

## 白板用词提示（3 组）

1. **不自然的短语**：longer times → *it takes longer*；hot and wet → *hot and humid*；basic action → *basic movement*（舞蹈）。
2. **缺冠词/物主词**：sign contract / extend contract → *sign a contract / extend your contract*；in free time → *in my free time*；guy in concert → *a guy at the concert*；create new → *create a new list*；view spot → *viewing spot*。
3. **事实/大小写**：taurus → *Taurus*，星座符号是 bull（公牛），不是 cow；**Mewali** 是巴厘语课堂记录，“不客气”这一释义未独立核验，故事中未翻译。
